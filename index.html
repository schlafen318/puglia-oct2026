Bike loops (detail)

1 · Valle d’Itria Acquedotto

~50–55 km · rolling · signature ride

Mix of the dedicated Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto Pugliese (~scenic aqueduct path) and quiet secondary roads through olive and trulli country. Links Alberobello, Locorotondo, and Cisternino — the postcard inland trio.

2 · Trulli & Martina Franca

~50–60 km · a bit more climbing

Country lanes past cone-roof houses toward Noci or Martina Franca’s baroque centre. More “town hopping,” slightly hillier than the aqueduct loop.

3 · Monopoli–Polignano coast

~45–55 km · flatter · go early

Adriatic cliffs, old harbours, gelato towns. Busier near the water — start early. Perfect contrast after inland rides.

Places & points of interest

Matera & the Sassi
One of the world’s oldest continually inhabited places: cave dwellings stacked in ravines (Sasso Caveoso & Barisano), now a UNESCO site and cinematic backdrop. Best on foot — uneven stone, stairs, viewpoints over the Gravina gorge.
Alberobello
UNESCO town of trulli — white limestone huts with conical stone roofs. Touristy at midday; magical early or late. Ideal e-bike hub for Valle d’Itria.
Locorotondo
Circular white hill town with a refined centro and wide views over vineyards and trulli roofs. Classic coffee/lunch stop on the aqueduct loop.
Cisternino
Quieter white town famous for evening fornelli (butcher-grill culture). Charming lanes; less crowded than Alberobello.
Martina Franca
Larger baroque town on the edge of the valley — palazzi, gates, and a lived-in centre. Good turnaround for Loop 2.
Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto Pugliese
Long-distance cycle path following the historic aqueduct. The Valle d’Itria section is the scenic heart: mostly dedicated path mixed with quiet roads (not 100% bike-only).
Ostuni
The “white city” on a hill above olive country — blinding white walls, cathedral, Adriatic views. Perfect mid-trip walk when shifting inland → coast.
Monopoli
Working harbour town with a lively old centre and nearby coves. Practical coast base: restaurants, character stays, easy access to Polignano and Ostuni.
Polignano a Mare
Cliff town over turquoise water; Lama Monachile beach cove is the iconic shot. Short, spectacular ride target from Monopoli.
Lecce
“Florence of the South” — exuberant Leccese baroque in golden stone (Santa Croce, Piazza Duomo). Walkable finale before the Brindisi hop.
Brindisi Airport (BDS)
Handy exit for Salento; ~40–50 min from Lecce. Drop the one-way hire car here before the Milan connector.
