Experiences

Recommended experiences woven into the days above — book timed or guided slots ahead where noted.

Casa Grotta

Sat 10 Oct · Matera base

Furnished cave dwelling (e.g. Vico Solitario) showing how families lived in the Sassi — beds, kitchens, cisterns carved into stone. Easy add during the walking day; no extra drive.

Cripta del Peccato Originale

Sat 10 Oct · ~15 km from Matera · book timed visit

Guided visit to vivid medieval rock frescoes — the so-called “Sistine of rock art.” Small groups, timed entry; book ahead.

Cantina MIR

Mon 12 Oct · Locorotondo · on Acquedotto loop

Hypogeum cellar at Piazza Aldo Moro 51. Taste Verdeca / Locorotondo DOC and reds underground. Book tasting; can trim the bike loop around the slot.

L’Acropoli di Puglia

Tue 13 Oct morning · Martina Franca · harvest season

Historic olive oil mill tour + tasting. Mid-October is crush time — mills feel especially alive. Pairs well with a short Martina Franca stroll.

Trullo cooking class

Tue 13 Oct afternoon/evening · near Alberobello · book ahead

Hands-on orecchiette (and friends) in a trullo — Maria’s, Cesarine, or similar. Seasonal produce; great counterpoint after the mill tasting.

Polignano boat cave tour

Thu 15 Oct · from Polignano · Mar–Oct · cancellable

~1.5–2.5h sea caves including Grotta Palazzese. Book refundable; October weather can cancel. Backup: Monopoli–Polignano bike loop + La Rotellina.

Ostuni Saturday market + olive walk

Conti Zecca

Bike loops (detail)

1 · Valle d’Itria Acquedotto

~50–55 km · rolling · signature ride · from Alberobello base · Mon 12 Oct

Mix of the dedicated Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto Pugliese (~scenic aqueduct path) and quiet secondary roads through olive and trulli country. Links Alberobello, Locorotondo, and Cisternino — the postcard inland trio. Slot Cantina MIR in Locorotondo; shorten if the tasting runs long.

2 · Trulli & Martina Franca

~50–60 km · a bit more climbing · optional / shorter on Tue 13 Oct

Country lanes past cone-roof houses toward Noci or Martina Franca’s baroque centre. With the olive mill + cooking class filling Tue 13, treat this as optional or a short spin only.

3 · Monopoli–Polignano coast

~45–55 km · flatter · backup for Thu 15 Oct if seas cancel the boat

Adriatic cliffs, old harbours, gelato towns. Busier near the water — start early. Primary plan for 15 Oct is the boat cave tour; keep this loop + La Rotellina as the rough-sea backup.

Places & points of interest

Colosseum (Rome)
Ancient amphitheatre and Rome’s icon — morning visit on 9 Oct for JJ before the Bari train. Pre-book a timed ticket (standard arena floor / underground options as preference and energy allow). Pair with a quick exterior pass of the Forum if time; prioritise the Colosseum itself over a full ancient-Rome day.
Matera & the Sassi
One of the world’s oldest continually inhabited places: cave dwellings stacked in ravines (Sasso Caveoso & Barisano), now a UNESCO site and cinematic backdrop. Best on foot — uneven stone, stairs, viewpoints over the Gravina gorge.
Casa Grotta (Matera)
Furnished cave home (examples on/near Vico Solitario) reconstructing daily life in the Sassi — cistern, mule stall, kitchen. Easy stop during the Sat 10 walking day.
Cripta del Peccato Originale
Rock-cut crypt ~15 km from Matera with extraordinary medieval frescoes (“Sistine of rock art”). Timed guided visits only — book ahead for Sat 10.
Alberobello
UNESCO town of trulli — white limestone huts with conical stone roofs. Touristy at midday; magical early or late. Ideal e-bike hub for Valle d’Itria and base for the cooking class.
Locorotondo
Circular white hill town with a refined centro and wide views over vineyards and trulli roofs. Classic coffee/lunch stop on the aqueduct loop — and home to Cantina MIR (Piazza Aldo Moro 51).
Cantina MIR (Locorotondo)
Hypogeum (underground) cellar tasting of Verdeca / Locorotondo DOC and reds. Book a slot; fits neatly into Loop 1.
Cisternino
Quieter white town famous for evening fornelli (butcher-grill culture). Charming lanes; less crowded than Alberobello.
Martina Franca
Larger baroque town on the edge of the valley — palazzi, gates, and a lived-in centre. Base for L’Acropoli di Puglia olive mill; optional short stroll on experiences day.
L’Acropoli di Puglia (Martina Franca)
Historic olive oil mill with tour and tasting. Especially worthwhile in mid-October harvest season.
Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto Pugliese
Long-distance cycle path following the historic aqueduct. The Valle d’Itria section is the scenic heart: mostly dedicated path mixed with quiet roads (not 100% bike-only).
Ostuni
The “white city” on a hill above olive country — blinding white walls, cathedral, Adriatic views. Sole visit: Sat 17 Saturday morning market + olive-grove walk from the Monopoli base (not a mid-trip stop on the Wed 14 inland→coast move).
Monopoli
Working harbour town with a lively old centre and nearby coves. Practical coast base: restaurants, character stays, easy access to Polignano and Ostuni.
Polignano a Mare
Cliff town over turquoise water; Lama Monachile beach cove is the iconic shot. Primary for the boat cave tour (Grotta Palazzese etc.); bike loop + La Rotellina if seas cancel.
Lecce
“Florence of the South” — exuberant Leccese baroque in golden stone (Santa Croce, Piazza Duomo). Walkable finale; evening food after the Conti Zecca day trip.
Conti Zecca (Leverano)
Salento winery ~30–40 min from Lecce — cellar tour and Negroamaro / Primitivo tasting. Book for Mon 19; keep the evening for Lecce.
Brindisi Airport (BDS)
Handy exit for Salento; ~40–50 min from Lecce. Drop the one-way hire car here before the Milan connector.
