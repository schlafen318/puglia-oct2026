Puglia · bike lanes, cellars & white towns · 8–21 Oct 2026

Two weeks south: a Rome night for JJ’s Colosseum morning, then Matera’s caves, e-bike loops through the Valle d’Itria, harbour days in Monopoli, and Lecce’s golden baroque on the way home. October means wine crush and olive harvest — the good kind of busy.

Day by day

Thu 8 Oct · Arrive Rome

Long night over Asia, soft landing in the city

CX293 Rome night
Stay: Rome hotel near Termini / Colosseo (proposed / TBD) · 1 night · favour Metro B + morning Termini access · ~€150–220

CX293 lifts from HKG T1 at 00:30 and touches FCO T3 at 07:35 — A350 nonstop, about thirteen hours. Leonardo Express or a taxi into town, check in, then nothing heroic: a short evening walk, early sleep. Tomorrow is JJ’s Colosseum morning, and you’ll want legs for it.

Fri 9 Oct · Rome → Matera

Colosseum first · then the long train south

Colosseum Rail Drive
Move day: check out Rome morning → check in Il Granile Contemporary Loft, Matera (night of 9; stay through Sat 10) · ~€180/night · Booking 9.6

Morning is for the amphitheatre — timed entry booked, priority stop for JJ. After that you’re bag-and-Termini people: the 14:58 Roma → Bari (arrive 19:20) is the sweet spot after a Colosseum morning; earlier 13:15–17:43 or later 15:57 / 18:05 trains work if the schedule slips. Sat 10 Oct · Matera

Cave streets, a furnished grotto, and the “Sistine of rock art”

Sassi Book crypt
Stay: still at Il Granile Contemporary Loft, Matera · 2nd night

Give the morning to Caveoso and Barisano — stairs, belvederes, rock churches — and duck into a furnished Casa Grotta (Vico Solitario or similar) to see how families actually lived in the stone: bed, kitchen, cistern, mule stall. Afternoon drive ~15 km / ~20 min to the Cripta del Peccato Originale for a timed guided look at those medieval frescoes. Dinner ideas: ARA or Era Convivial. No bike day; just walking and wonder.

Sun 11 Oct · Move inland

Matera hands you to the trulli

Drive ~70 km E-bike pickup
Move day: leave Matera morning (check out Il Granile) → check in Uniconico or Masseria Rosa, Alberobello (nights 11–13) · ~€150–160/night

About 70 km / 1h10–1h20 into cone-roof country. Check in, collect the e-bikes (deliver to Locorotondo or Alberobello), and take a short shakeout among the trulli before tomorrow’s classic loop.

Mon 12 Oct · Ride + tasting

Acquedotto loop · underground wine in Locorotondo

~50–56 km ~440–555 m↑ Book MIR
Stay: Uniconico or Masseria Rosa, Alberobello · continuing

Signature inland day: Alberobello → Locorotondo → Cisternino on the Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto and quiet lanes — white towns, dry-stone walls, olive rows. Expect roughly 50–56 km and ~440–555 m of climbing (rolling, not alpine). In Locorotondo, drop into Cantina MIR (Piazza Aldo Moro 51) for a hypogeum tasting of Verdeca / Locorotondo DOC and reds — book the slot; trim the ride if it runs long. Coffee and lunch in the white towns, then roll home.

Tue 13 Oct · Experiences day

Oil mill, baroque stroll, pasta in a trullo

Harvest season Cooking Optional short ride
Stay: Uniconico or Masseria Rosa, Alberobello · last night (check out Wed morning)

Morning at L’Acropoli di Puglia in Martina Franca — historic mill tour and tasting while the harvest is humming. Optional wander through Martina’s baroque centro between mill and lunch. Afternoon or evening: a trullo cooking class near Alberobello (Maria’s / Cesarine or similar) — orecchiette, seasonal produce, book ahead. Loop 2 (trulli & Martina Franca, roughly 35–40 km / ~300–360 m↑) stays optional or short; only if legs still want it after oil and pasta.

Wed 14 Oct · Coast move

Straight to Monopoli — Ostuni waits for Saturday

Drive ~22 km
Move day: leave Alberobello morning → check in Palazzo Veneziani, Monopoli (nights 14–17) · ~€156/night · 9.9

Alberobello → Monopoli direct ~22 km / ~25–30 min — no Ostuni detour today. Settle into Palazzo Veneziani and take the afternoon easy in the old town. The white city gets its own day on Sat 17 (market + olive-grove walk) — the sole Ostuni visit of the trip.

Thu 15 Oct · Polignano

Sea caves if the Adriatic cooperates

Primary: boat Backup: bike
Stay: Palazzo Veneziani, Monopoli · continuing

Primary plan: a boat cave tour from Polignano a Mare (Grotta Palazzese and neighbours) — typically 1.5–2.5h, season runs through October. Book cancellable; autumn seas can scrub a trip. If the boat’s off, ride Loop 3 Monopoli → Polignano (~28–32 km out-and-back, or up to ~45–50 km if you stretch the coastal spin; approx 90–180 m↑, nearly flat) and claim a pastry at La Rotellina. Either way, linger on the cliffs and Lama Monachile.

Fri 16 Oct · Susumaniello day

Tenute Rubino · then Monopoli harbour at dusk

Book winery Drive ~50–60 km
Stay: Palazzo Veneziani, Monopoli · continuing

October wants cellars more than a lounger day — so this free coast slot becomes a wine day. Drive from Monopoli toward Brindisi (~50–60 km / ~45–55 min) for Tenute Rubino, the house of Susumaniello: cellar tour and tasting (Wine & Cellar ~€35 / 1.5h, or Susumaniello Experience with food ~€85 / ~3h). Book ahead. Back in Monopoli by late afternoon for old-town lanes, the working harbour, and a long seafood dinner — no swim framing, just town and wine.

Sat 17 Oct · Sole Ostuni day

Saturday market and olive rows

Ostuni (only visit)
Stay: Palazzo Veneziani, Monopoli · last night (check out Sun morning)

This is the Ostuni visit — not a repeat; Wednesday went straight inland-to-coast. Drive from Monopoli (~30 km / ~35 min each way) for the Saturday morning market, then a short olive-grove walk in the countryside below the white walls. Arrange e-bike collection if it’s timed for Monopoli. If energy’s thin after yesterday’s cellar, keep the afternoon soft back at the harbour.

Mon 19 Oct · Lecce + winery

Casa Grotta

Furnished cave dwelling (e.g. Vico Solitario) showing how families lived in the Sassi — beds, kitchens, cisterns carved into stone. Easy add during the walking day; no extra drive.

Cripta del Peccato Originale

Sat 10 Oct · ~15 km from Matera · book timed visit

Guided visit to vivid medieval rock frescoes — the so-called “Sistine of rock art.” Small groups, timed entry; book ahead.

Cantina MIR

Mon 12 Oct · Locorotondo · on Acquedotto loop

Hypogeum cellar at Piazza Aldo Moro 51. Taste Verdeca / Locorotondo DOC and reds underground. Book tasting; can trim the bike loop around the slot.

L’Acropoli di Puglia

Historic olive oil mill tour + tasting. Mid-October is crush time — mills feel especially alive. Pairs well with a short Martina Franca stroll.

Bike loops (detail)

1 · Valle d’Itria Acquedotto

Distance: ~50–56 km · Elevation gain: approx. 440–555 m · Main draw: Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto + white towns (Alberobello–Locorotondo–Cisternino) · Cantina MIR stop
Signature ride · from Alberobello base · Mon 12 Oct · sources: Puglia e-bike (~50 km / 440 m), Wikiloc Valle d’Itria AQP loop (~56 km / 555 m)

Mix of the dedicated Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto Pugliese and quiet secondary roads through olive and trulli country. Links Alberobello, Locorotondo, and Cisternino — the postcard inland trio. Slot Cantina MIR in Locorotondo; shorten if the tasting runs long. Rolling terrain, not steep alpine climbing.

2 · Trulli & Martina Franca

Distance: ~35–40 km · Elevation gain: approx. 300–360 m · Main draw: trulli countryside lanes + Martina Franca’s baroque centro
Optional / shorter on Tue 13 Oct · sources: Komoot Itria Valley Villages loop (~39 km / 360 m), Bikemap Martina–Alberobello (~35 km / ~306 m)

Country lanes past cone-roof houses toward Martina Franca. With the olive mill + cooking class filling Tue 13, treat this as optional or a short spin only — the mill and pasta are the day’s heart.

3 · Monopoli–Polignano coast

Distance: ~28–32 km out-and-back (or ~45–50 km if you extend the coastal spin) · Elevation gain: approx. 90–180 m · Main draw: Adriatic cliff views, Polignano centro, Lama Monachile
Backup for Thu 15 Oct if seas cancel the boat · sources: Bikemap Monopoli–Polignano (~15 km / ~88 m one way), Probike north-coast loop via Polignano (~48 km, mostly flat)

Nearly flat coastal riding between harbours and cliffs. Busier near the water — start early. Primary plan for 15 Oct is the boat cave tour; keep this loop + La Rotellina as the rough-sea backup.

Places & points of interest

Colosseum (Rome)
Ancient amphitheatre and Rome’s icon — morning visit on 9 Oct for JJ before the Bari train. Pre-book a timed ticket. Pair with a quick exterior pass of the Forum if time; prioritise the Colosseum itself over a full ancient-Rome day.
Matera & the Sassi
One of the world’s oldest continually inhabited places: cave dwellings stacked in ravines (Sasso Caveoso & Barisano), now a UNESCO site. Best on foot — uneven stone, stairs, viewpoints over the Gravina gorge.
Casa Grotta (Matera)
Furnished cave home (examples on/near Vico Solitario) reconstructing daily life in the Sassi. Easy stop during the Sat 10 walking day.
Cripta del Peccato Originale
Rock-cut crypt ~15 km from Matera with extraordinary medieval frescoes (“Sistine of rock art”). Timed guided visits only — book ahead for Sat 10.
Alberobello
UNESCO town of trulli — white limestone huts with conical stone roofs. Touristy at midday; magical early or late. Ideal e-bike hub for Valle d’Itria and base for the cooking class.
Locorotondo
Circular white hill town with a refined centro and wide views over vineyards and trulli roofs. Classic stop on the aqueduct loop — and home to Cantina MIR (Piazza Aldo Moro 51).
Cantina MIR (Locorotondo)
Hypogeum cellar tasting of Verdeca / Locorotondo DOC and reds. Book a slot; fits neatly into Loop 1.
Cisternino
Quieter white town famous for evening fornelli (butcher-grill culture). Charming lanes; less crowded than Alberobello.
Martina Franca
Larger baroque town on the edge of the valley — palazzi, gates, and a lived-in centre. Base for L’Acropoli di Puglia olive mill; optional short stroll on experiences day.
L’Acropoli di Puglia (Martina Franca)
Historic olive oil mill with tour and tasting. Especially worthwhile in mid-October harvest season.
Ciclovia dell’Acquedotto Pugliese
Long-distance cycle path following the historic aqueduct. The Valle d’Itria section is the scenic heart: mostly dedicated path mixed with quiet roads.
Ostuni
The “white city” on a hill above olive country. Sole visit: Sat 17 Saturday morning market + olive-grove walk from the Monopoli base (not a mid-trip stop on the Wed 14 inland→coast move).
Monopoli
Working harbour town with a lively old centre. Practical coast base: restaurants, character stays, easy access to Polignano, Tenute Rubino (Brindisi), and Ostuni.
Polignano a Mare
Cliff town over turquoise water; Lama Monachile is the iconic cove. Primary for the boat cave tour; bike loop + La Rotellina if seas cancel.
Tenute Rubino (Brindisi)
Winery known for Susumaniello — cellar tours and tastings at Via Enrico Fermi. Day trip from Monopoli on Fri 16; book ahead.
Lecce
“Florence of the South” — exuberant Leccese baroque in golden stone (Santa Croce, Piazza Duomo). Walkable finale; evening food after the Conti Zecca day trip.
Conti Zecca (Leverano)
Salento winery ~30–40 min from Lecce — cellar tour and Negroamaro / Primitivo tasting. Book for Mon 19; keep the evening for Lecce.
Brindisi Airport (BDS)
Handy exit for Salento; ~40–50 min from Lecce. Drop the one-way hire car here before the Milan connector.
